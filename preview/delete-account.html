Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 28, 2026

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your workout history, progress data, and Pro subscription will be permanently removed.

What gets deleted

When you delete your LIFT account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account profile (name, email, avatar)
  • All workout history and completed exercise logs
  • Personal records, analytics, and progress data
  • Daily training consistency records
  • Your leaderboard ranking and community data
  • All locally cached data on your device

Option 1: Delete from within the App

  1. Open LIFT App Launch the LIFT app on your Android device.
  2. Go to Profile Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to Account Settings Scroll down to find the "Account" section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account" Tap the "Delete Account" option and confirm the deletion when prompted.
  5. Account Deleted Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly. Please include the email address associated with your LIFT account.

Send your deletion request to:

support@liftfitness.app

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Cancel your Subscription First

Deleting your LIFT account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. To avoid future charges, please cancel your subscription before deleting your account:

  • Open the Google Play Store app.
  • Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Find LIFT Pro and tap Cancel subscription.
