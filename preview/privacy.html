Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

LIFT ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the LIFT Workout & Gym Tracker application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account via email or Google Sign-In.
  • Fitness Data: Workout history, exercise logs, completed sets, weight, height, and fitness goals you enter in the app.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and anonymous usage analytics to improve app performance.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status managed through Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise your training experience and AI coaching.
  • To sync your workout data securely across devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • To process and manage your Pro subscription.
  • To display anonymised performance rankings on the community leaderboard.
  • To improve app features and fix issues through aggregated analytics.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore) with strict security rules ensuring only you can access your personal data. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and cloud database storage.
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management and purchase validation.
  • Google Play Billing — Payment processing for Pro subscriptions.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data as necessary to operate our services (e.g., syncing with Firebase). Anonymised, aggregated leaderboard data may be visible to other users.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Opt out of any optional data collection.

To exercise these rights, visit our Account Deletion page or contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

LIFT is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes within the app or by email.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@liftfitness.app
