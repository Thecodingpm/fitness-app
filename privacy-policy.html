Privacy Policy for LIFT - Smart Workout Tracker

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LIFT ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use LIFT, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you register or sign in using Firebase Authentication.
  • Fitness & Profile Data: Physical stats you choose to provide such as height, body weight, fitness goals, and workout experience level.
  • Workout Logs & Activity: Exercises performed, sets, repetitions, weights lifted, workout duration, and weekly consistency streaks.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic device identifiers, operating system version, and anonymous crash diagnostics to improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide and improve your fitness experience:

  • To track your workout progress and calculate weekly consistency streaks.
  • To provide custom exercise prescriptions and 3D biomechanical guidance.
  • To securely store your athlete profile across devices via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • To maintain app security, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical bugs.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers that adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore: For secure user authentication and encrypted database storage (Firebase Privacy Policy).
  • Expo / EAS: For app deployment and performance analytics.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit and AES-256 at rest) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • You can view, edit, or update your fitness profile directly inside the App settings.
  • You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated workout history at any time by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
